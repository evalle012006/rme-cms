# CMS Handover Notes

Operational notes for admins using the Rate My Estate CMS.

---

## Company Users (Builders / Developers)

Company Users are builder/developer accounts linked to a company. In the sidebar they appear under **Company Users** (Approved / Pending).

### How to create a Company User

1. Ensure the **company** already exists (Companies → Add, if needed).
2. Go to **Company Users → Approved** or **Pending**.
3. Click **Add Company User**.
4. Complete the Builder/Developer form (name, email, password, status, brand type, company) and click **Create**.

The new account appears on the Approved or Pending list according to the status chosen on the form.

**Users → Add** creates reviewer accounts only (not company users).

### Managing existing Company Users

- **Approve / move to pending:** use the status control on the Company Users lists.
- **Edit:** open **Edit** on a row to update profile and company details.
